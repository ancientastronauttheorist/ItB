reach **F8** through G8, and does the resulting original movement/effect sequence
agree with the Rust planner and replay?

effective profile, movement budget, and current occupancy when acquiring evidence.

filtering. The retained provenance leaves origin publication unresolved.
Original `Board:GetPath(start,start)` returns an empty vector by its reviewed
